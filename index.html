For Collectors

A Collector's Guide

A few notes for anyone starting — or growing — a collection.

01

Spotting quality vintage pottery & glass

Look for maker's marks on the underside. Hand-blown glass often carries small bubbles or a slight asymmetry that machine-made pieces simply don't have — those irregularities are evidence, not damage.

02

Caring for antique silver & metalware

Avoid abrasive cleaners. They strip a patina that took decades to form and can permanently reduce a piece's value. A soft cloth and a gentle polish is almost always enough.

03

Why provenance matters

An item's history — where it came from, who made it, how it was used — often matters as much as its condition. A documented origin is part of what you're buying.

04

Starting a themed collection

Most collectors find more satisfaction focusing on a single thread: one maker, one era, one region or one motif. A collection with a point of view beats an accumulation every time.
